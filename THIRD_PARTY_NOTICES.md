# Third-party notices

View Source Vibe ships data and depends on packages from other projects. The
licenses of all Dart/Flutter packages are listed in the app (About → Open
source licenses). Data that is bundled in the source tree:

## Open Cookie Database

- Used for: cookie names, categories and platforms in
  `lib/utils/cookie_db.g.dart` (generated by `tool/gen_cookie_db.dart`).
- Source: <https://github.com/jkwakman/Open-Cookie-Database> by Jack Kwakman
  and contributors.
- Version: see `tool/data/OCD_VERSION` (pinned commit; the vendored CSV is
  `tool/data/open-cookie-database.csv`, unmodified).
- License: Apache License 2.0, full text in
  `assets/licenses/open-cookie-database-LICENSE.txt` (also shown in the app).
- Changes made: the CSV is converted to Dart source, its categories are
  mapped to this app's categories (Marketing → advertising, Analytics →
  analytics, Functional/Personalization → functional, Security/Necessary →
  essential), some cookies of social platforms are classified as `social`,
  and entries are added or overridden in `lib/utils/cookie_overrides.dart`.

## Public Suffix List

- Used for: the definition of "same site" and "registrable domain" in
  `lib/utils/public_suffix.g.dart` (generated by
  `tool/gen_public_suffix.dart`, used by `lib/utils/site.dart`).
- Source: <https://publicsuffix.org/list/public_suffix_list.dat>, maintained
  by the Public Suffix List community.
- Version: see `tool/data/PSL_VERSION` (pinned; the vendored list is
  `tool/data/public_suffix_list.dat`, unmodified).
- License: Mozilla Public License 2.0, full text in
  `assets/licenses/public-suffix-list-LICENSE.txt` (also shown in the app).
- Changes made: the rules are converted to Dart source.
