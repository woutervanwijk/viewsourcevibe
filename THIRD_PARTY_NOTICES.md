## Open Cookie Database

- Used for: cookie names, categories and platforms in
  `lib/utils/cookie_db.g.dart` (generated by `tool/gen_cookie_db.dart`).
- Source: <https://github.com/jkwakman/Open-Cookie-Database> by Jack Kwakman
  and contributors.
- Version: see `tool/data/OCD_VERSION` (pinned commit; the vendored CSV is
  `tool/data/open-cookie-database.csv`, unmodified).
- License: Apache License 2.0, full text in
  `assets/licenses/open-cookie-database-LICENSE.txt` (also shown in the app).
- Changes made: the CSV is converted to Dart source, its categories are
  mapped to this app's categories (Marketing → advertising, Analytics →
  analytics, Functional/Personalization → functional, Security/Necessary →
  essential), some cookies of social platforms are classified as `social`,
  and entries are added or overridden in `lib/utils/cookie_overrides.dart`.
